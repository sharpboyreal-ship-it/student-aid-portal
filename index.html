About our platform

Helping students navigate information

Student Aid Portal is an independent platform designed to help users explore student funding information and organize funding inquiries. We are not affiliated with the U.S. Department of Education or any government agency. We do not guarantee scholarships, grants, or funding. Always verify eligibility and application requirements with the relevant funding provider.
